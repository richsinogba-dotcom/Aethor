01 The Story

Design should not be seen.
It should be felt — a sequence of moments that stay with you long after the screen goes dark.

AETHER is a small, deliberate collective of designers, motion artists, and engineers. We treat the viewport like a camera: framing, pacing, and depth are not decoration — they are the narrative.

From first concept to final render, we construct experiences that unfold with cinematic timing. Layered parallax, weighted typography, and restraint where it matters most.
